<h2 class="abt-sectitle">Beyond the Clock</h2>

<p>In digital systems, the clock defines rhythm, order, and synchronization. But not everything worth recording happens exactly on the clock edge.</p>

<p><strong>Beyond the Clock</strong> is about both sides of that boundary: the engineered systems inside the clock, and the personal systems beyond it&mdash;from silicon-level thinking to everyday tools and visual notes.</p>
